Glyph ranges for Noto Sans Regular and Noto Sans Bold (code points 0-2047),
in the MapLibre/Mapbox signed-distance-field PBF format, taken from the
OpenFreeMap font service (https://tiles.openfreemap.org/fonts/) on
2026-10-31 so the dashboard serves its own labels and fetches nothing from a
third party at run time.
